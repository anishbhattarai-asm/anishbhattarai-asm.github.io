About

Stream Panel keeps a YouTube live stream running around the clock on one free Oracle Cloud server, either a video playlist or a lofi image over a list of music tracks. Go live from OBS through it whenever you like, and the playlist carries on when you stop.

The panel has been tested end to end on Ubuntu 24.04 with real YouTube, Twitch, Kick and OBS. The one-click Oracle Cloud deploy has so far only been checked offline.

Highlights

  • Reconnects after network drops, restarts a frozen stream and resumes at the same second
  • Edit the playlist while live: add, delete, reorder and skip
  • Uses almost no CPU: every upload is converted once, then streamed as a plain copy
  • Stream to Twitch, Kick or Facebook at the same time
  • Alerts in Discord or on your phone
