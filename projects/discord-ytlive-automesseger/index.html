Discord YouTube Live Auto Messenger

Posts a message in your Discord server the moment you go live on YouTube, exactly once per stream.

JavaScriptMIT

View on GitHubDeploy to Render

About

No Discord bot and no database. It checks your channel with the YouTube API, posts through a Discord webhook, and sends exactly one message per stream even when the check runs every few minutes. The message text is yours to edit.

Set it up once in about 15 minutes on Render's free plan and never touch it again. None of the accounts involved asks for a payment card.

Highlights

  • One message per stream, never spam
  • No bot account and no database
  • Keeps itself awake on free hosting
  • One-click deploy to Render
